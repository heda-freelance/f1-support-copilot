# Account Settings

Your Acme account settings live under **Settings → Account** in the top-right user menu. From here you can update your profile, reset your password, and configure two-factor authentication (2FA).

## Reset your password

If you forgot your password, click **Forgot password?** on the login page. We will email a reset link to the address on file. The link expires in 30 minutes. If the email does not arrive within five minutes, check your spam folder, then contact support.

If you are signed in and want to change your password, go to **Settings → Account → Security** and click **Change password**. You will need your current password to confirm the change. Choose a password of at least 12 characters with a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols.

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)

We strongly recommend enabling 2FA on every account. To set up 2FA:

1. Go to **Settings → Account → Security**.
2. Click **Enable 2FA**.
3. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app such as 1Password, Authy, or Google Authenticator.
4. Enter the 6-digit code from your app to confirm.
5. Save the backup codes in a safe place — they let you recover access if you lose your device.

Once 2FA is enabled, you will be prompted for a code on every new device login. SSO users inherit MFA from their identity provider and do not need to enable 2FA separately in Acme.

## Edit your profile

Under **Settings → Account → Profile** you can update your display name, avatar, time zone, and notification email. Your username (the email you log in with) cannot be changed without contacting support — this prevents accidental loss of access to data tied to your old address.

## Delete your account

Self-serve account deletion is available under **Settings → Account → Danger zone**. Deletion is permanent and removes all data after a 30-day grace period. Workspace owners cannot delete an account that still has billing seats assigned — transfer ownership first.
