# Billing FAQ

This page covers Acme's pricing tiers, billing cycle, invoicing, and refund policy.

## Plan tiers

Acme offers four plans:

- **Free** — up to 3 users, 1 GB storage, community support.
- **Starter** — $12 per user per month, 50 GB storage, email support.
- **Business** — $29 per user per month, 500 GB storage, SSO, priority email support.
- **Enterprise** — custom pricing, unlimited storage, dedicated CSM, 24/7 phone support, signed DPA, SOC 2 report access.

You can compare features at **Settings → Billing → Compare plans**. Upgrades are prorated and take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing cycle.

## Billing cycle

All paid plans are billed monthly on the calendar day you first subscribed. For example, if you subscribe on the 14th, your billing cycle restarts on the 14th of every following month. Annual plans are billed once per year on the anniversary of the subscription start date and include a 15% discount.

You can switch from monthly to annual at any time under **Settings → Billing → Change cycle**. Switching from annual to monthly only takes effect at the next renewal.

## Invoicing

Invoices are emailed to the billing contact on file the same day a charge is made. You can also download the last 24 months of invoices from **Settings → Billing → Invoices**. To change the billing contact, go to **Settings → Billing → Contact**.

Enterprise customers can request net-30 invoicing instead of card billing. Contact your CSM to set this up.

## Refunds

We offer a full refund within 14 days of the first paid charge for monthly plans, no questions asked. After 14 days, monthly charges are non-refundable, but you can cancel at any time and the cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle — you keep access until then.

Annual plans are refundable on a prorated basis if cancelled within the first 30 days. After that, annual plans are non-refundable but renewable at the next anniversary.

To request a refund, email billing@acme.example with your workspace ID and the invoice number.
